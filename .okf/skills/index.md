# The skills

`skills/` is what a generic installer reads — the CLI at
[skills.sh](https://skills.sh) walks `skills/` in any git repository. Two of
them live here, and they are opposite in one important way.

* [The okf skill](okf-skill.md) - A **generated** copy. Its canonical tree is inside the gem, and editing it here is the mistake to avoid.
* [okf-principles](okf-principles.md) - **Canonical** here, and belongs to no gem: it documents a way of structuring instructions, not okf's code.
