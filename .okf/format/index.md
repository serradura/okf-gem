# The format

What Open Knowledge Format v0.2 is — the substrate the gem reads, validates, and
serves. The gem operates on this format; it does not extend it.

* [OKF v0.2](okf-format.md) - portable knowledge as a directory of Markdown + YAML frontmatter files.
* [OKF v0.2 — what changed from v0.1](okf-0-2.md) - the trust, provenance, lifecycle and attestation families, and the two retired spellings.
* [Frontmatter](frontmatter.md) - the YAML header on every concept (spec §4) and the gem's one YAML gateway.
* [Cross-links](cross-links.md) - plain Markdown links that become the knowledge graph's edges (spec §6).
* [Sources](citations.md) - the provenance family that keeps a bundle trustworthy (spec §5.1).
